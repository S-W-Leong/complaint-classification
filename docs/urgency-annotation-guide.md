# AI Urgency Annotation Guide

**Protocol version:** 2 (refined after pilot calibration)

## Scope and disclosure

This guide labels a CFPB complaint narrative by the urgency of the consumer's described situation, not by the product or the consumer's tone. Labels are generated by two AI annotators (`gpt-5.6-terra`, low reasoning) and are the final dataset labels. Any report using this dataset must disclose that provenance.

## Labels

- **High:** The narrative describes immediate or continuing material risk: an active loss of account access or essential funds; current unauthorized transactions/accounts or identity compromise; imminent housing, legal, safety, or severe financial harm; or a time-sensitive inability to stop the harm. A credible active identity-theft or unauthorized-transaction report is High even if the amount is not stated.
- **Medium:** A significant, unresolved problem needs priority attention, but no immediate severe harm is described. Examples include an unresolved credit-report dispute, delayed error investigation, accumulating loan interest, improper fees, debt-collection dispute, or account/service malfunction without immediate loss of essential access.
- **Low:** The issue is routine, resolved, informational, or minor and lacks an ongoing/material impact. Examples include a completed inquiry, a minor inconvenience, or dissatisfaction where the narrative gives no unresolved harm.

## Decision rules

1. Read the full narrative; do not label from product or issue fields alone.
2. Prioritize current risk and the inability to stop it. A severe event that is fully resolved is normally Medium or Low depending on remaining impact.
3. A credit inquiry alone, including an allegedly unauthorized inquiry, is **Low** unless the narrative establishes an active account, transaction, identity-compromise, or imminent-consequence risk.
4. An unresolved inaccurate, unverifiable, or reinvestigated credit-report item is **Medium** unless a High condition is explicit.
5. An active fraudulent account, current identity theft, or ongoing unauthorized transaction is **High**. A past event described as resolved, or a dispute without evidence of current loss/access risk, is **Medium**.
6. An inability to access needed account funds or an imminent eviction, foreclosure, lawsuit, arrest, utility shutoff, or comparable deadline is **High**; an ordinary unresolved money/service dispute is **Medium**.
7. If more than one condition occurs, choose the highest supported label.
8. Use `uncertain=true` only when the narrative genuinely supports two adjacent labels. Give a concise evidence-based rationale; never invent facts.
9. Confidence is a number from 0 to 1. Use 0.80–1.00 when the evidence clearly matches the definition, 0.55–0.79 for a close call, and below 0.55 only if marking uncertain.

## Output format

Each output CSV must have exactly these columns: `complaint_id`, `urgency_label`, `uncertain`, `confidence`, `rationale`. Use only `Low`, `Medium`, `High`; use lower-case `true` or `false` for uncertainty. Rationale should be one short sentence that refers only to facts in the narrative.
